Numbers quoted here come from `outputs/key_numbers.md` (regenerated by `run_all.py`). Always copy numbers from that

## 1. Research question and working title

*Why is the chance of failing the exit examination high? Institution type, examination round and repeated attempts
among medical graduates in Afghanistan, 2023 - 2026.*

Sub-questions: (1) how large is the failure problem and how far from the pass mark do candidates fall; (2) is it
linked to the institution the candidate graduated from; (3) does the examination round matter; (4) who retakes and
what is the chance of passing after a failure.

## 3. Statistical principles followed (put this in the Methods section)

1. **Data audit**: every raw row accounted for (kept or removed with one reason). 2. **Strict keys**: a round is identified
only by its label; one row per person per round is asserted. 3. **Pre-specified exclusions** (empty/zero score,
annulled result). 4. **Valid linking** with validation (field, institute, score agreement) and a sensitivity analysis.
5. **Correct uncertainty**: Wilson intervals, person-cluster bootstrap and cluster-robust SEs because a person can
appear several times. 6. **Effect sizes** beside p-values. 7. **Multiple comparisons**: Holm for round comparisons,
Benjamini-Hochberg for institutes. 8. **Case-mix adjustment** for institute comparisons (indirect standardisation),
shrinkage for small institutes. 9. **Censoring and survivorship** named explicitly (left-censoring before 2023,
right-censoring after 2026 R2, passers stop sitting). 10. **Reproducibility**: fixed seed, single config file,
one command, package versions in requirements.txt.

## 4. Wording rules

* Say "associated with", never "caused by": institutes differ in intake (entrance-exam scores, socio-economic background)
  and the data contain none of that.
* The 2026 R1 collapse is a **round effect** seen in all groups. The files do not say why (harder paper, marking,
  conditions). Ask the examination authority for the paper design before giving a reason.
* "Attempt number" counts only rounds in the data; a candidate may have sat the exam before 2023.
* Groups with 3+ failures are selected (people who keep failing); do not read their odds ratio as a causal effect.
* 2026 R2 contains only medicine; do not compare stomatology across all rounds.
* The 2024 gender column is male-only, so gender cannot be analysed.

## 5. Suggested paper structure

Abstract; Introduction (exit exam context, pass mark 216); Data and cleaning (Table 1 = audit); Linking and validation;
Methods (section 3 above); Results 1: rounds (B01-B03, B12); Results 2: institutions (A01-A06, A09); Results 3: retaking
(B04-B11); Models (A07/A08, B07/B08); Sensitivity; Discussion; Limitations; Ethics (names removed, hashed IDs,
only aggregated results published).

## 6. Data that would strengthen the paper

Section scores or question-level results, the paper difficulty/format per round, entrance-exam (concours) scores,
gender, time since graduation (only available for 2023), and the number of earlier exit-exam attempts.
